Rock paper scissors

Teach a neural network your hand shapes through the webcam, then play against an opponent that studies your habits and tries to predict your next throw. Everything runs in your browser; no video leaves your device.

Recording

Your camera stays off until you start it.

Keep your hand inside the dashed square, with a plain background behind it if you can.

1Teach your hand shapes

Hold each button while making that shape. Move your hand around a little: closer, farther, tilted. Aim for 60+ samples of each.

On a keyboard you can also hold R, P or S.

2Train the model

A small convolutional network learns from your samples. Takes about 10–30 seconds.
